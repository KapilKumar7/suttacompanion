Privacy Policy — Sutta Companion

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Overview

Sutta Companion is built on a simple principle: your data stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no crash reporting.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and we have no server that stores information about you.

What stays on your device

Everything you create or choose is stored only on your phone:

- Reading history, saved suttas, read-later list, notes and highlights
- Practice commitments and your daily practice log
- Meditation sessions (date, length, which sutta), your daily meditation goal and timer settings
- App settings such as theme, reading voice and reading speed

This data never leaves your device unless you export a backup file yourself.

Widgets and Shortcuts (iPhone)

The Daily Verse widget and the "Get Today's Verse Wallpaper" Shortcuts action run on your device. They show verses that are built into the app; nothing is sent anywhere.

Reading aloud and the optional HD voice

Suttas are read aloud on your device, using your phone's built-in voices or the optional HD voice. If you choose to download the HD voice (about 350 MB), the app downloads open-source voice model files from our download server (sutta-assets.pomogolo.com, hosted by Cloudflare). The voice then runs entirely on your phone; the text you listen to is never sent anywhere. You can delete the HD voice at any time in Settings → Reading Voice.

App updates

The app checks for and downloads improvements from Expo's update service (expo.dev). This download, like the HD voice download, sends no personal data. As with any internet request, the hosting providers (Cloudflare, Expo) receive standard technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the files; we do not receive, store or use it.

Notifications and meditation bells

Daily reminders and meditation-timer bells are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to any server to deliver them.

Backup & Restore

The backup feature exports a local file to wherever you choose (Files, iCloud, email, etc.). We never receive or store this file.

Permissions

- Notifications: for your reminders and meditation bells (you can decline).
- Background audio (iPhone) / media playback (Android): so reading continues when the screen is off.
- Exact alarms (Android): so meditation bells ring on time.
- The app does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
